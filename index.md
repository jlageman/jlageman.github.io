I am a cognitive computational neuroscientist with a strong interest in goal-directed action, perception, decision-making, and sensorimotor learning. 

During my PhD I focused on the interactions of action and perception in the brain, deriving and testing novel predictions using original experimental designs and computational modelling of behavioral and EEG data. My aim is to contribute to an advanced understanding of how the brain processes sensory information and makes adaptive decisions.
